How to read this

A forecast is only worth something if it beats the free alternatives. Persistence assumes nothing changes from the launch hour, which is hard to beat in the first few hours. Climatology assumes the site does what it usually does at this hour, which is hard to beat several days out. Skill is the fractional improvement over a baseline: positive is better, zero is no better, negative is worse. Coverage is how often the observation fell inside the 10th to 90th percentile band, which should be near 80 percent.
