{# One short paragraph explaining how to read the page, so a first-time reader is never guessing what a band or a confidence word means. #}

How to read this

  • The big number is the day's high, the small one its low.
  • Rain is the total expected across the whole day. "None" means less than 0.2 mm, which a rain gauge would not register.
  • Wind is the strongest sustained wind expected. Gusts are shown separately only when they are well above it.
  • Confidence comes from how much the forecast range varies, not from a guess. High means the number is reliable; low means treat it as a rough direction, not a figure to plan tightly around.
  • Confidence drops the further ahead you look. The first day or two is always the most trustworthy part of any forecast.
