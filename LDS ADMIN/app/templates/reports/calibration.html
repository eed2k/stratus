Antenna resonance

The sensor's receiving antenna is a tuned LC circuit. Its resonant frequency must sit at {{ '{:,}'.format(calibration.target_hz) }} Hz within {{ calibration.tolerance_pct }} percent, a permitted deviation of {{ '{:,}'.format(calibration.tolerance_hz) }} Hz, or the sensor's distance estimate degrades. The detector measures the frequency and adjusts an internal tuning capacitor to correct it.

Oscillator recalibration

The sensor's internal RC oscillators drift with temperature. The detector recalibrates them when the enclosure temperature moves past its threshold, and on a routine interval regardless. A high proportion of temperature-triggered recalibrations points to a thermally exposed installation rather than to a fault.
