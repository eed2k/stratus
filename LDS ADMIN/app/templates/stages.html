Stages are distance bands that escalate. A strike raises the nearest stage that covers it, and only that one, so one flash never sends someone three messages. With stages at 30, 20 and 10 km a strike at 8 km raises 10 km; one at 25 km raises 30 km; one at 36 km raises nothing.

While no stages are defined, alerts fall back to each group's own distance threshold on the Groups page. Define a stage and the stages take over: the stage decides the distance, and the group it names decides who is told.
