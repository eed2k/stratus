Storm activity

Recorded strikes grouped into five distance bands, nearest first, over the selected window. Each band gives its strike count with the peak, mean and lowest intensity recorded in that band. A cloud starts flashing on the first strike in its band and keeps flashing until 12 hours pass with no further activity there, so a lit cloud means the cell is still live. The count follows the window you select, which is why a lit cloud can read zero on a short window. The AS3935 reports distance up to 40 km in 15 discrete steps (±4 km) but does not measure bearing, so no direction is shown anywhere. Intensity is a relative sensor value for comparing strikes with each other, not joules.

Storm track

Distance of every strike against time. The approach and retreat of each cell is what the five bands above summarize. Vertical axis is distance from the detector, so lower is closer.
