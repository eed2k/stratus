Storm animation preview

Two separate animations, both already live on the panel.
1. Drifting thundercloud sky — pure CSS (@keyframes storm-drift on .storm-sky). Always running, deliberately low opacity so it never costs the plot contrast.
2. Strike pulse — @keyframes strike-pulse on .strike-new, applied by storm-view.js to strikes it has not seen before. On the live panel it only fires when a genuinely new strike arrives, which is why an idle dashboard shows no movement.
Both are suppressed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — turn that on in your OS and reload to check.

1. The cloud sky on its own, isolated

This is just .storm-sky with nothing on top of it, so the drift is easy to see. Give it 20–30 seconds: the movement is intentionally slow.
