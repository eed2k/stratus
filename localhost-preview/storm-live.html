Recorded strikes grouped into five distance bands, nearest first, over the selected window. Each band gives its strike count with the peak, mean and lowest intensity recorded in that band, and flashes more often the busier it is. The AS3935 reports distance up to 40 km in 15 discrete steps (±4 km) but does not measure bearing, so no direction is shown anywhere. Intensity is a relative sensor value for comparing strikes with each other, not joules.
